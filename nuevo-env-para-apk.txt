Valores nuevos para el .env de la APK 3.0.5
==============================================
Primero despliega el servidor y copia tu URL.
Ejemplo: https://urbani-proxy-xxxx.onrender.com
Reemplaza TU-SERVIDOR por esa URL en las líneas de abajo.

API_MIGRATION=https://TU-SERVIDOR/migration
API_GETS=https://TU-SERVIDOR/gets
API_COUPON=https://TU-SERVIDOR/coupon
API_SEGMENTED=https://TU-SERVIDOR/segmented
API_STATIC=https://TU-SERVIDOR/static
API_EVENTS=https://TU-SERVIDOR/events

Los demás valores se restauran directo a Urbani real
(no pasan por tu servidor):
API_AUTH=https://auth-prod.urbani.io
API_PAYMENTS=https://api-payment.urbani.io
API_MEDIA_MANAGER=http://media-manager-prod.urbani.io
API_Maps=https://urb-gps-prod.urbiaccess.io
API_kmz_Stops=https://urb-gps-prod.urbiaccess.io/app/kmz
API_kmz_Routes=https://urb-gps-prod.urbiaccess.io/app/kmz/line
API_kmz_Close_Routes=https://urb-gps-prod.urbiaccess.io/app/aprox/branch
API_Radius_Routes=https://urb-gps-prod.urbiaccess.io/app/g/radius
API_MMC=https://back.appmemuevo.io
API_STP=https://stp-app.urbani.io
API_SURVEY=https://epayments-prod.urbani.io
API_CONEKTA=https://api.conekta.io
API_LIVENESS=https://auth-prod.urbani.io
API_ELASTIC=https://app.urbani.io
RUTAEXPRESS_UNLIMITED=https://static-prod.urbani.io

Nota: este servidor solo reenvía a Urbani real.
No crea saldo, no inventa cupones ni viajes.
